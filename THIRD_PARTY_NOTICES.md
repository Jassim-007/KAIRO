## MuscleMap

`@musclemap/react`, `@musclemap/core`, and `@musclemap/assets` provide the anatomical SVG muscle maps. Their MIT license covers the package code and traced SVG muscle paths. The upstream project states its bundled demo body photographs are AI-generated and MIT-licensed; this app uses the vector maps and does not include those photos.
